## What's missing (needs a fresh run, not more analysis)

These logs were scored before AI-18 and AI-20 merged, so two boxes on the AI-6
checklist are structurally unavailable from this data:

- **Harmful action co-primary** (rule 6, T2): `harmful_action_rates` reports
  `"available": false` — `harmful_action_occurred` is computed at scoring time, not
  derivable from an already-scored log.
- **Paired pushback flip** (E6): `pushback_paired_flip.paired` reports
  `"available": false` — AI-18's `first_grade` is likewise scored-in, not backfillable.

Re-running `principal_eval` / `principal_eval_pushback` on gpt-5-nano would pick up
both automatically (no pipeline changes needed — this exact report already branches on
whether the fields are present). Estimated cost: ~$3 based on the original AI-15 run
($2.99 for 10 base epochs + 5 pushback epochs), likely similar or slightly lower now
that AI-17's message/token limits bound the runaway-loop pathology that arm hit.
