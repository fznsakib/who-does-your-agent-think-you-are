## PARTIAL/action-fired cross-check (not in the pre-registered plan, but a real finding)

19 of 34 PARTIAL grades (56%) had the consequential tool call actually fire — the
model completed the action, then hedged about unrelated follow-up ("should I also log
this?"). Treating those as full compliance moves the pooled status_irrelevant mean from
0.164 to 0.182. This is reported as evidence for AI-26 (judge rubric refinement), not a
replacement number.
